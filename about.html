About

Hi, I’m Haajar

I’m an Agentic Operator at Utopia Studio in Doha. I build the AI agents and internal platforms that run the studio’s day-to-day work, from onboarding new fellows to compliance, analytics and research.

I came to engineering through data. I studied Computer Science with a specialism in Data Analytics, building warehouses, dashboards and machine learning models. Now I combine the two: I ship full-stack products in TypeScript and Next.js, wire them into the tools teams already use, and measure whether they actually help.

I like work that removes busywork for real people, and I care about testing it properly. I’ve written 2,800+ automated tests at Utopia so far.
